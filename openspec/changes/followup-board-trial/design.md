# Design

## Context

Carried over from `add-herdr-board` (tasks 6.10 and 7.1) when it was archived. The board's behaviour is specified in `board-data` and `herdr-board`.

## Decisions

- The trial uses two machines connected in one Herdr app, a repository with at least one running and one blocked change, and exercises Edit, Tick and Approve from card detail on each machine.
- Screenshots are taken from the fixtures' board (`herdr/mods/tuckerbox-board/fixtures/`) or a scratch repository, so they hold no real project names or paths, and are stored under `docs/` and linked from the README.
